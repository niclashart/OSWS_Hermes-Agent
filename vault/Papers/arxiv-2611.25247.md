## Core idea
BM25 wins explicit citations (94% R@20), dense wins implicit (16%); their rankings recover disjoint implicit references, so fusion reaches 22% implicit R@20 — near cross-encoder reranking (26%) without its inference cost. Evidence for hybrid BM25+dense fusion as a RAG-NIMBF axis.
