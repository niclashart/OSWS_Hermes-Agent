# Large-Scale Biomedical RAG

**Axis:** [[Retrieval]] / [[Evaluation]] · **Score:** 7/10 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26339) · [PDF](https://github.com/niclashart/OSWS_Hermes-Agent/blob/main/papers/2610.26339/paper.pdf)

## Core idea
Controlled evaluation of corpus scale, document representation (abstract / flat full-text / hierarchical), and reranking for biomedical RAG, with latency and storage cost.

## Results
> "Hierarchical representation matched or outperformed flat full-text retrieval in most end-to-end comparisons while requiring 67% fewer indexed units and 44-56% less storage" (Abstract)
