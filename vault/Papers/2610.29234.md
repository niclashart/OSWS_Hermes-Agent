# LMVU: Marginal Viewpoint Utility for Budgeted Evidence Selection

**Axis:** [[Reranking]] · **Score:** 7/10 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29234) · [PDF](https://github.com/niclashart/OSWS_Hermes-Agent/blob/main/papers/2610.29234/paper.pdf)

## Core idea
Budgeted evidence selection by marginal viewpoint coverage: a lightweight ranker scores candidates by the viewpoints they add to the current selection under a fixed context budget.

## Results
> "LMVU achieves gains of up to 0.047 and 0.052 points in RVC$_1$ and RVC$_2$, respectively, over the strongest non-oracle baseline for each benchmark and metric, and reaches the exact viewpoint-breadth ceiling on two benchmarks" (Abstract)
