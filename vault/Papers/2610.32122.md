# Semantically Rich, Metrically Affect-Blind: A Geometric Blind Spot in Modern Text Embedders

**Axis:** [[Embedding]] · **Score:** 7/10 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32122) · [PDF](https://github.com/niclashart/OSWS_Hermes-Agent/blob/main/papers/2610.32122/paper.pdf)

## Core idea
Sentiment is decodable from embedder outputs (up to 91.3%) but absent from the cosine metric; a small CPU-trained contrastive injection head recovers retrieval MAP for affect tasks.

## Results
> "The head improves every encoder released since 2022 on full-data probe accuracy (up to +5.8 points), retrieval MAP (up to +26.0) and polarity margin (7-61x)." (Abstract / Results)
