# Graph Topology Meets Energy-Driven Pathfinding (ThreadRAG)

**Axis:** [[New-Techniques]] · **Score:** 8/10 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29933) · [PDF](https://github.com/niclashart/OSWS_Hermes-Agent/blob/main/papers/2610.29933/paper.pdf)

## Core idea
Graph-RAG without triplet loss of context: Semantic Topology Graph over context-intact Semantic Nodes (narrative, semantic, lexical edges) plus Energy-Driven Pathfinding that linearises query-relevant subgraphs into coherent textual evidence paths. Findings of EMNLP 2026.

## Results
> "it achieves a 14.4% EM improvement over text-centric methods on HotpotQA and surpasses the best baseline by 5.8% EM on 2WikiMultihop, while reducing token cost to 23.3% of the strongest baseline" (Abstract)
