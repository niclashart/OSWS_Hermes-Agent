# Active Learning of Approximate LLM Ranking for Energy-Efficient Retrieval of Long Queries

**Axis:** [[Query-Transformation]] · **Score:** 7/10 · [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16488)

## Core idea
Learned predictor actively trained with sparse LLM labels approximates LLM reranking at 3.3% of the energy.

## Results
> "LAAM achieves 87% of average P@10 achieved by DeepSeek's reranking (0.459 vs. 0.526), while requiring only 3.3% of the per-query energy consumption." (Abstract)
