# Factual Yet One-Sided: Stance Poisoning and Evidence Balancing in Retrieval-Augmented Generation

**Axis:** [[New-Techniques]] · **Score:** 7/10 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34294) · [PDF](https://github.com/niclashart/OSWS_Hermes-Agent/blob/main/papers/2610.34294/paper.pdf)

## Core idea
Correct-fact stance poisoning shifts RAG stance monotonically; Stance-Balancing Middleware (detection + 2+2+1 balancing + debiasing prompt) between retriever and generator reduces deviation.

## Results
> "Across all eight concentration-direction settings, the complete method reduced the magnitude of stance deviation by an estimated 0.106 to 0.350; the six settings at poisoning shares of 11.9%-18.9% remained significant after Holm adjustment (adjusted p <= 0.009)." (Abstract / Results)
