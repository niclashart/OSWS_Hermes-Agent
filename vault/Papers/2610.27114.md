# Joint Constraint Aggregation (JCA)

**Axis:** [[New-Techniques]] · **Score:** 7/10 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27114) · [PDF](https://github.com/niclashart/OSWS_Hermes-Agent/blob/main/papers/2610.27114/paper.pdf)

## Core idea
Answer-selection stage comparing all candidates against the same question-derived requirements; counteracts retrieval skew toward partially matching but frequently mentioned competing answers.

## Results
> "Across three evaluation settings and three instruction-tuned backbones, JCA improves overall accuracy, and its advantage grows when retrieval favors a competing answer" (Abstract)
