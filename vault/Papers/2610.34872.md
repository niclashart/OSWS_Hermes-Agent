# Adaptive Retrieval, Selective Answers: Evaluating Conformal Factuality in Agentic RAG

**Axis:** [[Evaluation]] · **Score:** 7/10 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34872) · [PDF](https://github.com/niclashart/OSWS_Hermes-Agent/blob/main/papers/2610.34872/paper.pdf)

## Core idea
Conformal factuality filtering after agentic RAG: stricter targets raise full-support rates but cut answer availability sharply.

## Results
> "At the 95% target, non-empty response coverage ranges from 17.23% to 60.17%, claim retention from 10.72% to 43.16%, and full support among non-empty outputs from 89.14% to 95.56%." (Abstract / Results)
