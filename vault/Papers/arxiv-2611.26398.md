# Agentic AI Ecosystem for Cross-Project Knowledge (Water Governance & Climate)

**Axis:** [[New-Techniques]] · **Score:** 7/10 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26398) · [PDF](https://github.com/niclashart/OSWS_Hermes-Agent/blob/main/papers/arxiv-2611.26398/paper.pdf)

## Core idea
ReAct-style controller allocates a retrieval budget across domain-specific collections and accumulates evidence until a stopping criterion is met; two domain-fine-tuned embedders. "improves Mean Reciprocal Rank by 26.7% (relative)" over a single-domain embedding baseline. Evidence for retrieval-budget allocation / evidence-sufficiency stopping as an agentic retrieval axis.
