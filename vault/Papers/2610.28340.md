# DeclineCraft: RAG Abstention Benchmark

**Axis:** [[Datasets]] / [[Evaluation]] · **Score:** 8/10 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28340) · [PDF](https://github.com/niclashart/OSWS_Hermes-Agent/blob/main/papers/2610.28340/paper.pdf)

## Core idea
Dynamic abstention benchmark (Minecraft post-cutoff features, parametric-exposure audits) measuring Correct Abstention Rate across 27 LLMs; code open.

## Results
> "Iterative RAG ... significantly lowers CAR versus single-shot RAG in 30 of 40 configurations (mean 46.4%), with little change in Answer Accuracy (68.5% to 69.3%)" (Abstract)
