# System One models Are Not Better Rerankers

**Axis:** [[Reranking]] · **Score:** 8/10 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32318) · [PDF](https://github.com/niclashart/OSWS_Hermes-Agent/blob/main/papers/2610.32318/paper.pdf)

## Core idea
Decision fine-tuned (System One) models never beat reranker fine-tunes of the same backbones on 10 TREC DL/BEIR collections; calibration does not compensate for worse ranking.

## Results
> "From 0.6B to 9B, reranker fine-tuning wins on every backbone by 5 to 29 points nDCG@10, also on zero-shot collections, and at 27B the two tie." (Abstract / Results)
