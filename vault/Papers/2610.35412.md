# Read the Route: Attention-Steered Hypergraph Retrieval for Multi-hop RAG

**Axis:** [[New-Techniques]] · **Score:** 7/10 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35412) · [PDF](https://github.com/niclashart/OSWS_Hermes-Agent/blob/main/papers/2610.35412/paper.pdf)

## Core idea
Attention addressing heads resolve implicit entities in one forward pass; Personalized PageRank on a hypergraph steers multi-hop retrieval with no query-time generation.

## Results
> "HyperAddress outperforms the strongest baseline by 3.5 points of Recall@5 and 7.4 points of All@5 on average, with gains that grow with the number of hops. It answers more than twice as fast and builds its index at half the cost." (Abstract / Results)
