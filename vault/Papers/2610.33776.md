# TriageRAG: Failure-Aware Self-Training for Adapting Retrieval-Augmented Language Models to Specialized Domains

**Axis:** [[New-Techniques]] · **Score:** 7/10 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33776) · [PDF](https://github.com/niclashart/OSWS_Hermes-Agent/blob/main/papers/2610.33776/paper.pdf)

## Core idea
Failure-aware self-training: probe student with/without context, triage QA pairs into four failure categories, route each to targeted training signal.

## Results
> "TriageRAG outperforms a budget-matched SimRAG by 1.2-6.4 points and is the only method that recovers the closed-book level in every case where retrieval hurts the base model." (Abstract / Results)
