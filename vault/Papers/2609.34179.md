# RAGWarrant: Evidence-Preserving Governance for RAG Policy Promotion Under Quality, Cost, Latency, and Risk Constraints

**Axis:** [[Evaluation]] · **Score:** 7/10 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34179) · [PDF](https://github.com/niclashart/OSWS_Hermes-Agent/blob/main/papers/2609.34179/paper.pdf)

## Core idea
Open-source promotion-control framework: normalizes evaluator outputs, applies predeclared quality/hard-risk gates, and emits auditable PROMOTE/BLOCK/REJECT/INCONCLUSIVE decisions on RAG policy changes.

## Results
> "On HotpotQA, operational savings were blocked because answer quality fell beyond the declared margin. A bounded CRAG study selected a lower-cost quality-tied policy, but related generative gains were unstable and a held-out guardrail failed closed." (Abstract)
