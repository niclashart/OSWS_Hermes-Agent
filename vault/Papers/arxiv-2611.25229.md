# LSP-RAG: Latent Scoring Perspectives for Knowledge Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation

**Axis:** [[New-Techniques]] · **Score:** 7/10 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25229) · [PDF](https://github.com/niclashart/OSWS_Hermes-Agent/blob/main/papers/arxiv-2611.25229/paper.pdf)

## Core idea
KG-RAG multi-hop triplet retrieval scored by K latent perspectives (perspective-specific soft prompts) plus a hop-updated contextual soft prompt; lightweight frozen backbone (Flan-T5-3B best: WebQSP 88.59), trained with contrastive loss, beats ChatGPT-retrieval baselines.
