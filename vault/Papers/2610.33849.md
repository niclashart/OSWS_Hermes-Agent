# Ranking Is Not Deciding: Benchmark Shortcuts and Operating-Point Collapse in Query-Time RAG Poisoning Defenses

**Axis:** [[Datasets]] · **Score:** 7/10 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33849) · [PDF](https://github.com/niclashart/OSWS_Hermes-Agent/blob/main/papers/2610.33849/paper.pdf)

## Core idea
Poisoning-defense benchmark results driven by poison-construction cues; removing the prefix collapses decisions (RAGuard AUROC 0.953 to 0.758) while ranking survives. Demands construction-profile + FPR reporting.

## Results
> "Deleting only that prefix from the attack authors own passages (0.93 still retrieved for their target) takes RAGuard, a published content filter we re-implement, from AUROC 0.953 to 0.758 and its miss rate from 0.191 to 0.763." (Abstract / Results)
