# Scan or Index? Long-Document RAG

**Axis:** [[Retrieval]] · **Score:** 8/10 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28957) · [PDF](https://github.com/niclashart/OSWS_Hermes-Agent/blob/main/papers/2610.28957/paper.pdf)

## Core idea
11 retrieval configurations (lexical/dense/hybrid/full-scan) compared on QASPER + textbooks with fixed generator; evidence coverage vs answer accuracy vs cost trade-off.

## Results
> "Hybrid + Jev achieves the highest observed evidence coverage (94.1%), compared with 88.7% for Jev full scan and 81.3% for OpenAI dense retrieval" (Abstract); "Hybrid + Decisions achieves the highest observed book Answer Accuracy among retrieval methods (0.892), despite recovering less reference evidence" (Abstract)
