# Preference Is Not Position: Reranking Evaluation on NevIR

**Axis:** [[Reranking]] · **Score:** 7/10 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26260) · [PDF](https://github.com/niclashart/OSWS_Hermes-Agent/blob/main/papers/2610.26260/paper.pdf)

## Core idea
Joint evaluation of pairwise preference and list position on negation-sensitive NevIR pairs in fixed hybrid candidate pools; LLM reranker (Qwen3-14B-AWQ) vs BGE cross-encoder.

## Results
> "Qwen3-14B-AWQ with fusion tie-breaking reaches 77.45% strict preference ... and preferred-passage MRR of 0.7509. A BGE cross-encoder reaches 69.77% strict preference and MRR of 0.7404" (Abstract)
