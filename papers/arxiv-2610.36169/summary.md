# Don't Pick a Winner: Knowledge Conflicts Need a Prior, Not a Rule (arxiv-2610.36169)

**Core idea:** In context-vs-parametric-knowledge conflicts, any fixed resolution rule is beaten somewhere; the paper derives a decision-theoretic model showing fixed rules are linear in the share of reliable contexts while the optimum is convex, arguing for an adaptive prior.

**Key result (verbatim):** "the score of any fixed rule is linear in the share of reliable contexts while the best attainable score is convex, so every fixed rule is beaten wherever it is not exactly optimal" (Abstract)
