# Reasoning over Non-Parametric Memory in Multilingual LLMs (arxiv-2611.06715)

**Score:** 8/10 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06715)

## Core idea
Rel-Bloom: a controlled multilingual diagnostic (450 MCQs from 30 Wikidata relations) that supplies facts directly in the prompt — no retriever — at five Bloom-inspired cognitive levels and under Factual/Counterfactual/Neutralized veracity conditions. It measures whether LLMs reason over supplied evidence rather than memorized associations, which is the core assumption RAG makes about context-conditioned generation. 43 models, six languages, 226,800 predictions.

## Key results
- > "accuracy drops sharply from Apply to Analyze in every evaluated open-weight model series, reaching 47.2 percentage points for the Large Granite bucket" (Abstract / Sec. 4.2)
- > "Gemini 3.1 Pro averages 99.5% on the other four levels but 60.7% on Evaluate" (Abstract / Sec. 4.2)
- > "Counterfactual contexts are generally harder than Factual ones" (Abstract / Sec. 4.3)
