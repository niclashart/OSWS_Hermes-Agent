## Per axis

### Chunking (4 relevant)
Literature: query-aware evidence units (Tessera, 2610.37080), learned single-objective chunkers (QESChunker,
2610.36557), update-cost-aware content-defined chunking (2610.31019). NIMBF covers fixed/token/markdown/semantic
strategies. Missing: content-defined chunking (new-axis proposed), structure-completed evidence units,
and any evaluation of *index-update cost* (recompute fraction under document revisions).

### Embedding (4 relevant)
Contextual embedding evaluation beyond the gold chunk (2610.38854), geometric blind spots of text embedders
(2610.32122), xPRT cross-passage pretraining (2610.40122). NIMBF can slot any HF embedder, but has no
fine-tuned/trained-retriever axis (explicitly not expressible) and no per-chunk-context embedding evaluation.

### Retrieval (5 relevant) + Query-Transformation (4 relevant)
Cost-aware retrieval ("scan or index", 2610.28957; when-is-more-retrieval-worthwhile, 2610.37358),
coverage-aware decomposition for long-form answers (2610.40314), staged linguistic seeding expansion (2609.00844),
incremental multi-hop decomposition (DeFT-RAG, 2610.37826). NIMBF has similarity/MMR, multi-hop follow-ups and
HyDE. Missing: query decomposition as a first-class axis, token-budgeted context selection, cost-vs-quality
trade-off reporting (only load-test efficiency exists).

### Reranking (9 relevant) — highest-signal axis
The single 10/10 of the month (2609.38473): reranking and late interaction are the strongest quality levers
under a fixed generator. LLM-as-reranker (Glance 2610.40454), decision-model rerankers (2610.38090),
agent-context-aware rerankers (ACARE 2610.35840). NIMBF supports cross-encoders only; late interaction
(ColBERT) and LLM rerankers are explicitly not expressible. A ColBERT provider was proposed in `impl/2026-W44`
(partial → proposal); LLM reranking remains a gap.

### New-Techniques (26 relevant) — largest bucket
Three clusters:
1. **Graph RAG** (≥ 9 papers): ThreadRAG (2610.29933, +14.4% EM), SpectRAG (2610.36991), TACO-Graph
   (2610.36650), SGUR (2610.30611), GBOR-RAG (2610.30710), TopoGraphRAG-Bench, HyperDoc (2610.35542).
   Not expressible; the biggest structural gap.
2. **Pre-generation evidence gating / factuality control**: RINSE (2609.37469, 0.837 vs 0.746 prior best),
   conformal factuality control (2609.38222, 95.8–97.2% supported), RAGScope (2609.39075), verifier cascades
   (2610.20634). NIMBF has no gate/between-retrieval-and-generation component.
3. **Agentic search regulation**: MAPS parallel reasoning (2610.29792), OpenSaC search-as-code (2610.25779),
   MEMORA committee reranking with memory (2610.25282), proactive memory recall (2610.34507).
   Only MCP agentic tool use is expressible today.

### Evaluation (19 relevant) — second-largest bucket
Compositionality (2610.29722), copy-ceiling input-exposure control (2609.24885 — implemented as NIMBF metric),
penalty-aware knowledge-gap evaluation (2608.26385), retrieval-coverage auditing (2609.24122), LLM-judge blind
spots (2610.15938), Bayesian unified eval (2608.24753), judge debiasing (2609.12439). NIMBF's RAGAS +
custom-metric surface covers much of this; missing: abstention/knowledge-gap metrics, compositionality,
input-exposure controls beyond copy-ceiling, judge-bias diagnostics.

### Datasets (10 relevant)
Abstention benchmark (DeclineCraft 2610.28340), stress-testing under knowledge churn (RAGStress 2610.04691),
structured RAG (perovskites 2610.25575), synthetic-vs-authentic query distributions (2609.14579),
coverage-guided eval-set generation (CoverSynth 2610.15409). NIMBF's `hf-generic`/`jsonl` adapters can ingest
most; missing: benchmark *suite* support (multiple datasets per experiment is a matrix, but no churn/revision
datasets) and abstention ground truth.

## Gaps (ranked by evidence × feasibility)

1. **Graph RAG / weighted chunk-graph retrieval** — 9+ papers with strong quantitative gains (ThreadRAG +14.4% EM,
   SpectRAG +9.4pp over Graph-RAG). NIMBF: nothing. Feasible: medium-high (new retrieval strategy + graph store).
2. **Token-budgeted context selection / compression** — SpectRAG, 2610.40314, 2610.29234, hf-caveman compressor.
   Explicitly not expressible. Feasible: medium (context-selection stage between reranker and prompt).
3. **Pre-generation evidence-sufficiency gate** — RINSE, RAGScope, conformal factuality, verifier cascade.
   Consistent 5–10pp hallucination gains at low cost (some CPU-only). Feasible: medium (new optional stage + metric).
4. **LLM / late-interaction reranking** — 10/10 paper identifies reranking as the top lever; NIMBF is
   cross-encoder-only. ColBERT provider already designed in impl/2026-W44; LLM reranker provider still open.
5. **Index-update / churn evaluation dimension** — CIPBench, 2610.31019 (57–68% vs 43–54% recompute).
   New-axis proposal exists (arxiv-2610.31019); needs revision-pair ingestion + `recompute_fraction` metric.
6. **Long-context vs RAG routing** — EDAR (2609.35831): 97.4% accuracy at −70.7% tokens. Not expressible;
   needs a `retrieval_mode: routed` value and a routing-policy component. Feasibility: medium.
7. **Abstention / knowledge-gap metrics** — DeclineCraft, 2608.26385. Only refusal detection exists today.
   Feasible: small-medium (custom metrics extension).

## Suggested Related Work sentences (for the next RAG-NIMBF paper)

- "Recent work shows graph-structured retrieval over chunk graphs and semantic topologies improves multi-hop
  consistency [2610.29933; 2610.36991; 2610.36650], but such systems lack a configuration-space benchmark;
  RAG-NIMBF fills this by treating retrieval strategy, reranking and chunking as composable axes."
- "Controlled comparisons identify reranking and late interaction as the dominant quality levers under a fixed
  generator [2609.38473], complementing pre-generation evidence-sufficiency gating [2609.37469] and conformal
  factuality control [2609.38222], all of which RAG-NIMBF can express as single-axis ablations."

## Suggested Future Work sentences

- "Future work will add an index-update axis to measure recompute cost under document revisions, following
  evidence that chunking strategies differ by up to 25 pp in recomputation workload at equal retrieval quality
  [2610.31019] and that index-maintenance filtering is itself benchmarkable [2610.25108]."
- "We plan to extend the evaluation stage with abstention and knowledge-gap metrics [2610.28340; 2608.26385]
  and with judge-bias diagnostics [2610.15938; 2609.12439], and to add graph-RAG and token-budgeted context
  selection as first-class retrieval strategies."
